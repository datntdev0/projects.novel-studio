The bar is emerald for done items and red for failed items, which sit right after the done part; a paused bar is amber. Active rows offer Pause or Resume and Cancel; finished rows offer exactly Rerun, Delete and Open logs. Item rows: pending, running (attempt n/3), completed, failed (translated error, attempts, raw CLI text under Details); no item actions in R1.1.
